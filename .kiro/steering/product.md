# Ghostline

A browser walkie-talkie for small crews, reviving push-to-talk radio through WebRTC. Entering Kiroween 2026, Resurrection category.

Keep the experience focused: create a channel, share the invite, hold to talk, release to listen. Maximum six active members; four-hour rooms; fifteen-second transmissions; one speaker at a time.

Use the existing olive, cream and phosphor-green design. English product copy. Responsive and keyboard accessible. Never fabricate participants, connection status or audio activity. The visual waveform indicates the floor owner, not a measured audio waveform.

Never claim offline operation, guaranteed cross-network connectivity, emergency reliability or end-to-end identity verification. WebRTC audio is not recorded by this app. Signaling metadata is temporarily stored in D1. STUN-only connections may fail on restrictive networks; TURN configuration is optional and still needs production setup.

Never claim a Kiro feature was used unless it actually ran. Preserve an honest account of tool contributions in docs/development-log.md.
